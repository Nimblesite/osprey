Readable text is mostly arithmetic. Keep lines between 60 and 75 characters, give body text a line height of about 1.6, and pick a type scale with a ratio you can hold in your head.

## A scale of five

Display, heading, subheading, body and caption. Anything more is decoration, and decoration is where inconsistency hides.

**Rule of thumb:** if two sizes look almost the same, delete one of them.
