Perception is a music studio (DAW) written in Osprey. It patches synth voices, builds effects from mathematical formulas, mixes through side-chained buses and renders songs to WAV, all on the [`@osprey/synth`](https://github.com/Nimblesite/osprey/tree/main/src/osprey/packages/synth) library.

Perception lives in its own repository. Its guides, downloads and videos are on the Perception website.

**[Go to the Perception website →](https://nimblesite.github.io/perception/)**
