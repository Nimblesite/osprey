## Goal

Close the proof stack end to end: the kernel checks the pinned Lean core and Mathlib from `.lean` source without a Lean executable; other type systems plug in beside it as disciplines; production formulas are written in Osprey and their properties, and the compiler's own output, are checked by the kernel; and the workbench authors, checks and teaches proofs on every host. Lean compatibility is never a precondition for another discipline, no timing gates CI, and no verdict is reported unless the Lean toolchain or Osprey's front end gave it.
