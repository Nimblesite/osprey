Formula keypad

A visible symbol keyboard immediately below the lesson’s Your formula field. The implication arrow is a wide, named key, so finding it does not depend on knowing Unicode or a shortcut.

  • Paper & Slate tokens
  • STIX Two Math · STIX Two Text · Geist
  • 44 px minimum targets
  • Light, Slate and narrow views

Static design review. This page does not edit, submit or check a formula. It shows the intended presentation and key states; the runtime contract is in formula-keypad.md. No proof verdict is claimed here.
