## Exact generation prompt

Create a finished app icon artwork for a beautiful playful native audio casting player, designed to remain immediately recognizable at tiny sizes. A single luminous coral/apricot orb with one simple dark-indigo musical note cutout at its center, and two short thick curved broadcast arcs above its upper right edge integrated into the silhouette. Charming old-school MP3-player spirit, premium contemporary precision. Very simple bold geometry, generous negative space, restrained dimensional shading like softly polished coloured glass, lavender subtle highlight, no intricate detail. Square composition, centered isolated mark with generous padding, no app-icon rounded-square container (the OS supplies its mask), transparent background. No text, no letters, no wordmark, no mockup, no device, no extra objects, no watermark. Strong silhouette and contrast, not a lightbulb illustration with a screw base. This is the standalone product mark for the casting player, not a replacement for the Osprey language logo.
